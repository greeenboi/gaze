## Day 2

So Sunday was the day I was able to attend and it was a blast tbh. I was so happy to see so much linux representation this year, We had Redhat, FreeBSD. OpenSuse, Debian and Ubuntu.

We also had so many oldies like XMPP , Openstreetmap and F-Droid showcasing and standing for better open standards especially in this day and age ([support android](https://keepandroidopen.org/)). 

We had a good time walking around the booths meeting old faces like the bruno team who recognized me 😄 and having a very intellectual talk about our homelabbing with other enthusiasts. 

For the sake of not boring you with all the talks i have attended, I will be steering this post towards more of the people i met and the things i talked about..

<br/>

So First up is as i mentioned above, I met with the bruno team, and they were glad to see me, it seems I had made an impression on them last year, they remembered me. So I picked up on what we were discussing last year. I believe that was support for websockets (they support that and alot more!). We then spoke a bit about our current situation, work n all.. along the way I asked them if they were planning on bringing about testing suites or anything along those lines.
They mentioned that it was in the works. Now that I am in a more corporate soln, a test suite inbuilt inside would give me alot more say if i want my team to switch to bruno for our projects.

Afterwards we left to explore a couple more booths, we saw an interesting open source company, whose product is essentially a better federated and decentralised platform for git. To put it in simple terms , they built a self hostable platform (similar to gitea/gitlabs) which will register itself as a node where the main site can also see these nodes and projects. I liked the concept, but i felt i wanted to wait and see where this project goes.

By now a couple of the events we wanted to see were starting, we checked them out, had lunch and went back to see the remaining booths.

We went to all the linux distro booths, we had alot of fun talking shop. We also checked out the Redhat sponsor booth, They were taking clips for their socials most likely and supposedly a gift if you show something interesting. So I showed them the BattleShip game "GridLock" I am building with gnome (to be release on flatpak). They loved the experience and the gameplay so much, they gave me a mug and so many badges and stickers, o7 Thank you fedora enthusiasts!!

Thats all for the main highlights, There was alot of drama in the main hall when they spoke about AI in Education sectors. I think it would be best if you saw the livestreams for yourself on the website.

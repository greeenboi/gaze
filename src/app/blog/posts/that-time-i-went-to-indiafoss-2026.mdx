## Day 2

So Sunday was the day I was able to attend and it was a blast tbh. I was so happy to see so much linux representation this year, We had Redhat, FreeBSD. OpenSuse, Debian and Ubuntu.

We also had so many oldies like XMPP , Openstreetmap and F-Droid showcasing and standing for better open standards especially in this day and age ([support android](https://keepandroidopen.org/)). 

We had a good time walking around the booths meeting old faces like the bruno team who recognized me 😄 and having a very intellectual talk about our homelabbing with other enthusiasts. 

For the sake of not boring you with all the talks i have attended, I will be steering this post towards more of the people i met and the things i talked about..

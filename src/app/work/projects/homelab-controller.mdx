## Homelab Controller

A GTK4 / libadwaita desktop app for driving a Proxmox VE homelab.

Unlocks with My keyring, pick At Home or Remote, and get live host stats, a list of VMs and containers with power controls, one-click shortcuts to your self-hosted services, and an SSH terminal — all in one window.

Check it out at my [gitea](https://gitea.suvan.page/greeenboi/Homelab-Controller)
